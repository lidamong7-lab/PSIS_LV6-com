📖 Midterm Reading Passage: Healthy Living & Mindful Driving

Making wise choices in daily life is essential for maintaining personal health and ensuring public safety. When it comes to nutrition, understanding food categories helps us eat better. Whole grains, such as brown rice and oats, retain essential nutrients and fiber compared to refined grains. Additionally, vegetables like potatoes and cassava are classified as starchy vegetables, which provide energy but should be eaten in balance with green vegetables and poultry like chicken or turkey. Many individuals adopt specific diets to lose weight or avoid diseases like obesity. However, when offered food that doesn't fit a diet or causes discomfort, politely declining with phrases like "It doesn't agree with me" helps manage social situations smoothly.

Similarly, safety choices on the road are vital. Unsafe habits like tailgating (driving too close behind another vehicle) and weaving through traffic significantly increase the risk of collisions. A minor accident, often called a fender bender, can easily escalate into a severe pileup or head-on crash if drivers are distracted by texting or speeding. Responsible drivers always observe the speed limit and use turn signals. If everyone practiced mindful eating and cautious driving, overall well-being and road safety would improve dramatically.
